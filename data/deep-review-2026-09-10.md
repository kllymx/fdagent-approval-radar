# Review of expanded Astra investigations

Review date: September 10, 2026. This log covers newly completed investigations from the expanded research batch. Earlier investigations are documented in [recorded-run-review.md](recorded-run-review.md).

The reviewer is a separate agent from the agent conducting each investigation. Generated findings, assessments, and conclusions are not edited during review. A material unsupported claim is grounds to withhold a run and request clarification or a new investigation; the original response remains in the local audit trail.

## Scope and publication decisions

Checks concentrate on consequential clinical results, safety findings, manufacturing and inspection scope, current application identity, dated supersession, and whether the qualitative assessment follows from its cited evidence. Numerical claims and important source language are checked against retrieved original-source text where available. Registry and citation metadata alone do not establish clinical outcomes. Previous Astra summaries are not independent corroboration.

**Publishable within reviewed scope** means no material error was found in the claims checked. It does not mean every sentence or linked document was independently verified, the public record is complete, a clinical expert endorsed the conclusions, or the assessment predicts FDA's decision accurately. Reading a run's cached original-source extraction is disclosed as such; it is not a fresh independent retrieval. No patient-level reanalysis, confidential submission review, or predictive calibration is performed.

The evidence cutoff remains September 10, 2026. Reported action dates are review targets, and completed approvals are observed outcomes. Safety or manufacturing information that could not be retrieved remains unknown. Publication preparation may sanitize source metadata and excerpts, but must preserve the generated analysis and disclose that sanitation.

### Atezolizumab / adjuvant dMMR colon cancer — 90914235-74d9-4ea7-8a55-93bd6362fad7

**Decision: Publishable within reviewed scope.** Strong DFS evidence is retained; the mixed category reflects observed additional toxicity rather than treating unknown CMC or immature survival as proof of failure.

- FDA's cached Hybreza approval notice supports prior formulation bridging and its coprimary PK criterion. This does not independently establish colon efficacy or present supplement/site clearance. The critical commentary is explicitly attributed interpretation rather than an FDA objection.

**Limits:** This check used cached registry results, the publication abstract and FDA formulation notice. The full original paper/SAP, persistent-toxicity data, exposure-adjusted analysis, current supplement numbers and FDA benefit–risk conclusions were not independently reviewed.
