# Review of expanded Astra investigations

Review date: September 10, 2026. This log covers newly completed investigations from the expanded research batch. Earlier investigations are documented in [recorded-run-review.md](recorded-run-review.md).

The reviewer is a separate agent from the agent conducting each investigation. Generated findings, assessments, and conclusions are not edited during review. A material unsupported claim is grounds to withhold a run and request clarification or a new investigation; the original response remains in the local audit trail.

## Scope and publication decisions

Checks concentrate on consequential clinical results, safety findings, manufacturing and inspection scope, current application identity, dated supersession, and whether the qualitative assessment follows from its cited evidence. Numerical claims and important source language are checked against retrieved original-source text where available. Registry and citation metadata alone do not establish clinical outcomes. Previous Astra summaries are not independent corroboration.

**Publishable within reviewed scope** means no material error was found in the claims checked. It does not mean every sentence or linked document was independently verified, the public record is complete, a clinical expert endorsed the conclusions, or the assessment predicts FDA's decision accurately. Reading a run's cached original-source extraction is disclosed as such; it is not a fresh independent retrieval. No patient-level reanalysis, confidential submission review, or predictive calibration is performed.

The evidence cutoff remains September 10, 2026. Reported action dates are review targets, and completed approvals are observed outcomes. Safety or manufacturing information that could not be retrieved remains unknown. Publication preparation may sanitize source metadata and excerpts, but must preserve the generated analysis and disclose that sanitation.

## Completed reviews

Reviews are added below only after an actual completed report and its relevant evidence have been inspected.

### Floretyrosine / glioma imaging — 14f1d098-81cb-4806-908c-f20f1e1aad8a

**Decision: Publishable within reviewed scope.** The concerning clinical assessment is supported by a candidate-specific FDA objection, rather than inferred from missing information.

- Inspected the cached FDA CRL for NDA 218592. The sensitivity figures of 58–73% and specificity figures of 36–51% are ranges of lower confidence bounds across readers, as the report expressly states. The letter also supports the reference-standard, single-center, missing-data and dose-applicability objections and the option for robust confirmatory evidence. Its redacted product-quality section does not justify a specific current CMC allegation.
- Checked the current NCT07238322 JSON: 253 actual participants, retrospective imaging data with prospective analysis, January 23, 2026 completion and no posted results. Neither enrollment nor completion proves diagnostic performance. The report appropriately leaves the study's exact role in the resubmission unverified.
- Cached September 2025, March 2026 and April 2026 Telix disclosures support the described consultation, new submission and reported September 11 target. They express sponsor views about adequacy; the report does not promote them into FDA acceptance of the clinical remedy. The PharmaLogic disclosure establishes a historical product relationship, not site clearance. The separate TLX250 letter is correctly excluded from Pixclara manufacturing conclusions.

**Limits:** No new confirmatory accuracy tables, integrated safety data, current site assessment or confidential FDA feedback were available. Source checks used cached public-source extractions; the August target reaffirmation remained an attributed catalog summary. The adverse outlook is a qualitative interpretation of the unresolved public evidence, not a forecast of the next action.

### Zilurgisertib / FOP — cb5e3505-3df1-4b3b-b342-0d944a6068b1

**Decision: Publishable within reviewed scope.** The report preserves both the primary-endpoint failure and evidence of biological activity without equating nominal secondary results with confirmatory success.

- Checked the cached joint sponsor results against the current NCT05090891 JSON. The primary endpoint is new-lesion occurrence at week 24; the reported p=0.0986 is nonsignificant. The source really reports 5 placebo patients as 16.7% despite a randomized-arm header of 31; the report correctly raises the denominator issue instead of silently repairing it.
- Lesion-volume means, dispersion, nominal p-values and week-48 observations match the retrieved table. Extension observations come from the same cohort after crossover, not an independent controlled replication. The registry's estimated enrollment of 98 includes additional cohorts and is not the 63-person pivotal comparison.
- The supportive safety factor rests on actual sponsor-reported controlled-period observations, including no adverse-event-driven discontinuations or dose reductions. The report identifies missing serious-event counts, longer-term exposure and subgroup detail, rather than treating this as proof of comprehensive safety. Its manufacturing unknown excludes unverified BIMO leads. The CTEXLI approval metadata supports a separate completed application, not a zilurgisertib approval predictor.

**Limits:** Clinical source verification used the cached sponsor release and registry, not a full study report or FDA efficacy review. The relevant SEC narrative was not successfully retrieved by the run, and that limit is disclosed; the inspected sponsor results independently establish the primary miss. Concerning versus mixed remains a qualitative materiality judgment, not a validated rejection probability.

### UX111 / MPS IIIA — 839be2e3-037f-4c9d-99ec-d040cbcf780b

**Decision: Publishable within reviewed scope.** The mixed assessment preserves substantive early-stage clinical evidence alongside an actual unresolved CMC objection and limits on broader efficacy claims.

- Cached FDA letter `crl-b590235141ac` supports systemic inspection deficiencies, inadequate corrective responses, possible verification inspections, deferred lot-release review and continued evaluation of clinical adequacy. The subsequent incomplete-response letter is a separate submission-completeness event; the April acceptance supersedes that status without proving CMC resolution. Named Andelyn/Bedford manufacturing relationships are present in the acceptance disclosure, but the report does not assign redacted findings to those sites.
- The February 2026 sponsor release supports the 33-person exposure population, 27-person high-dose efficacy set, 17-person earlier-stage analysis, cognitive effect of +23.2 points, gross-motor p=0.070 and 63.98% CSF-HS exposure reduction. The report correctly distinguishes external natural-history comparisons, overlapping follow-up and maximum versus median observation time.
- Inspected original NCT04088734 results. The five-person advanced-disease study was terminated for lack of efficacy. Three patients experienced serious events; the attribution/time table really includes one severe, possibly related SAE at 180 days to under 12 months. Its name cannot be identified from that cell. Day-30 biomarker change, missing month-12 observations, clinician global assessments, liver-enzyme counts and zero deaths in the stated mortality window match. These results are not substituted for the earlier-stage population or called proof of drug causality.

**Limits:** Checks used cached primary-source text and registry JSON. The full natural-history comparison, locked analysis plan, integrated safety tables, current corrective-action dossier and FDA disposition of the replacement submission were unavailable. The distinction between different historical endpoint descriptions warrants diligence but does not establish improper endpoint switching.

### Ifinatamab deruxtecan / ES-SCLC — 719dbabb-e741-4194-ae66-d6bc5c2f76ad

**Decision: Publishable within reviewed scope.** The clinical/safety assessment follows actual results, with comparator and manufacturing boundaries preserved.

- Inspected the cached original JCO paper, DOI `10.1200/JCO-25-02142`, including methods, efficacy and adverse-event sections. The 137-person 12-mg/kg analysis, ORR 48.2% (39.6–56.9), median response duration 5.3 months and second-line subset of 32 match. Dose randomization is not a standard-treatment comparison. The paper expressly says its intervals are unadjusted for multiplicity and not hypothesis tests.
- The severe, serious and discontinuation percentages match Table 5 with all-cause and related categories kept separate. Six investigator-attributed treatment-related deaths and two adjudicated fatal ILD cases are different classifications and are not added. Exposure, ILD incidence and onset statistics match; this is a documented safety concern rather than a generic ADC warning.
- FDA's cached November 2025 tarlatamab notice confirms traditional approval and randomized survival evidence, superseding older accelerated-only descriptions. The report does not infer comparative superiority across trials. The separate patritumab CRL and May 2025 withdrawal disclosure support the historical example while expressly excluding it as proof of an ifinatamab manufacturing defect. Sponsor manufacturing responsibility is not site clearance.

**Limits:** The reviewer did not inspect the lower-dose safety supplement, patient-level data, exact FDA acceptance correspondence or product-specific CMC package. The proposed accelerated-versus-traditional pathway remains explicitly unverified. Comparative confirmation is planned, not completed; the supplied trial dates are estimates.
