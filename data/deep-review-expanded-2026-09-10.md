The Western follow-up update did not update locked Asian data. Extended PFS HR 0.57 and response-duration values also match. The report does not turn these later analyses into a repaired prespecified OS success.

Grade ≥3 treatment-related events 50%/42%, serious treatment-related events 61/33 (28%/15%), specified cytopenias and four/five treatment-related deaths match. No excess fatal-event claim is made. The report preserves the difference between the paper's rounded 1% severe hemorrhage and sponsor wording below 1%, without inventing exact counts or exposure adjustment.

The output correctly separates contractual supply/technology transfer from CGMP findings and rejects a BIMO name match as manufacturing evidence.
