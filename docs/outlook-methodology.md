# Astra evidence outlook

The outlook is a categorical appraisal of the evidence supplied to Astra for a specific drug, indication, and review episode. It is **not an estimated probability of FDA approval**, an expected approval date, a clinical recommendation, or an investment score. The four factor states describe evidence direction, not four independent chances of success.

Assessments must identify their model, generation time, evidence cutoff, and input scope. A catalog-packet assessment reads attributed source summaries and candidate-specific material from any supplied investigation. It does not imply a fresh search, a review of every linked document, or access to the confidential application. A previous Astra investigation is a synthesis whose original source references remain necessary; it is not independent corroboration of those sources.

## Overall categories

| Category         | Required basis                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | What it does not mean                                                                                                       |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Favorable**    | Substantive benefit evidence relevant to the requested indication supports the application case, and the supplied packet does not identify an unresolved material obstacle that outweighs it. Identify the particular positive evidence and the most important remaining unknown. | Approval is more likely than not; safety or manufacturing has been cleared; the public packet is complete.                  |
| **Mixed**        | The packet contains material competing evidence, or a documented obstacle has a credible mitigation whose application-specific acceptance remains unresolved. Explain the actual tension.                                                                                         | Some factors are unknown, or an arbitrary number of signals are negative.                                                   |
| **Concerning**   | A specific, material adverse finding remains unresolved in the current episode and substantially weakens the case. Explain why later evidence does not establish its resolution.                                                                                                  | FDA will reject the application, or an old setback permanently determines the current outlook.                              |
| **Insufficient** | The packet lacks decision-bearing outcome or assessment evidence needed to appraise the application, or its relevance cannot be established. Identify the missing evidence.                                                                                                       | The drug is ineffective, unsafe, unlikely to be approved, or necessarily less promising than a better-documented candidate. |

Unknown safety or manufacturing information **does not automatically downgrade favorable to mixed**. Conversely, filing acceptance, priority review, a near-term action target, or the existence of a randomized phase 3 study cannot alone support favorable. Do not manufacture a distribution of categories to make the interface appear balanced.

An observed outcome takes precedence over a prospective outlook. In the September 10, 2026 catalog, zilganersen has a recorded September 3 approval. Display **Approved** as its observed status; do not present it as a successful forecast or include it among still-pending prediction examples.

## Four evidence factors

For each factor, return `supportive`, `mixed`, `concern`, or `unknown`, a short candidate-specific reason, and the source IDs supporting any factual claims. An unknown factor that explicitly describes missing packet information may have no source IDs; every non-unknown factor needs supporting references. Unknown is an affirmative statement about the packet's limits, not a hidden negative score.

| Factor            | Supportive evidence                                                                                                                                                                      | Material concerns and boundaries                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clinical**      | Reported results with a relevant endpoint and analysis population, interpreted in the context of design, comparator, effect, and regulatory pathway. Attribute sponsor-reported results. | A missed pivotal endpoint, an unresolved analysis-plan or multiplicity issue, conflicting replication, or a material mismatch with the proposed indication can warrant mixed/concern. Study registration, enrollment, completion, or publication indexing alone leaves efficacy unknown. Single-arm evidence is not automatically inadequate, but does not establish randomized superiority. |
| **Safety**        | Candidate- and population-specific safety findings whose exposure and limitations are described, or a relevant favorable assessment explicitly attributed to its source.                 | A documented material safety issue can warrant concern. No safety discussion is unknown. Generic reassurance, an approved different indication, a clean sponsor inspection, or an absence of spontaneous reports does not establish safety in the proposed population, dose, or setting.                                                                                                     |
| **Manufacturing** | Concrete product-and-site-specific progress or a relevant favorable facility assessment, with inspection scope and the remaining application assessment distinguished.                   | Track the actual site, product, and current review episode. Inventory, reserved capacity, a sponsor's BIMO inspection, or a manufacturer's unrelated inspection does not establish CGMP or application clearance. A removed site must not be treated as proof that its replacement is deficient.                                                                                             |
| **Regulatory**    | Filing acceptance, documented review progress, or resolution of a particular issue supports the procedural position within its stated scope.                                             | Acceptance is not a finding of effectiveness. Priority review changes the pathway, not approval odds. An extension establishes a timing change; it is not automatically an unfavorable benefit–risk finding. An advisory-committee concern, unresolved eligibility dispute, or FDA deficiency must be assessed against later amendments and the current indication.                          |

Use `mixed` within a factor when both sides are material to that factor. Do not use it merely because supportive evidence is incomplete. Use `concern` only for an identified adverse issue, with its present relevance stated. A source summary saying that results exist, without reporting what they show, cannot support an efficacy label.

## Required reasoning order

1. Confirm the drug, formulation/combination, indication, population, application type, current status, and cutoff. Distinguish a pending supplement from an already-approved product.
2. Read dated evidence in order. Identify which earlier statements were superseded and which issues remain open. Repeated disclosures of the same fact are not independent confirming evidence.
3. Separate observed results from regulatory procedure, clinical inference, and missing information. Keep primary and secondary endpoints, prespecified and post-hoc analyses, and intent-to-treat and selected populations distinct.
4. Assign the four factor states with source-linked reasons. Source prominence and the number of positive headlines are not weights. Direct FDA findings and the sponsor's description of those findings may require different levels of attribution.
5. Choose the overall category through materiality and relevance, **not voting or arithmetic**. State the decisive positive or negative fact and the single missing fact most capable of changing the outlook.

Clinical benefit and risk must match the target application. A failed sleep-apnea endpoint does not erase a different trial's hypertension result. An oncology approval does not settle an endocrine resubmission. Overseas approval is not a US decision. A planned confirmatory trial is not completed evidence. Immature overall survival is different from a failed final prespecified survival analysis.

## Display and validation

Use a discrete **Evidence outlook** badge and four plainly labeled factor indicators. Unknown factors should appear neutral/gray. Keep the evidence cutoff and a short explanation visible, with source links and a route to the full investigation. A compact label such as “Favorable evidence” is clearer than “Likely approval.”

Do not convert the categories or factor counts into a percentage, progress meter, betting line, or approval-date forecast. A green badge cannot silently mean “FDA has cleared this.” Reported action dates remain in their own calendar/status display. Existing empirical cohort references must stay separate from these judgments and must not be multiplied, adjusted, or presented as candidate probabilities.

Structural validation should require the exact candidate identity, allowed categories, four distinct factors, a nonempty rationale, and source references that belong to the supplied packet. Semantic review must additionally check source entailment, temporal relevance, missing-data handling, and whether a different indication or inspection program was substituted. Structural validity alone does not validate the outlook. No retrospective predictive accuracy or calibration claim is made for this rubric.

An unsupported category should be withheld and reassessed from adequate evidence, retaining the original response in the local audit trail. Do not silently edit a generated category into the desired answer. In the initial September 10 batch, lorundrostat's favorable label relied on summaries saying that trials supported the application without reporting hypertension outcomes; that packet did not justify clinical support. A new investigation must read the actual results before assigning a replacement outlook.
